ABOUT NIXXMOTOAXX

Built for Riders

NIXXMOTOAXX is a fictional motorcycle accessory business created for our Event-Driven Programming project.


Our business focuses on providing practical, stylish, and affordable motorcycle accessories for everyday riders.


We created NIXXMOTOAXX with the idea of making motorcycle accessories easier to browse and order through a simple and interactive website.

Our Mission

To provide riders with accessible and practical motorcycle accessories.

Our Vision

To become a trusted motorcycle accessory brand for everyday riders.

Our Goal

To create a convenient online shopping experience for motorcycle enthusiasts.
